## 🎙️ Pre-Flight Recording Checklist (Devpost Rubric)

- [ ] **Tab Ready:** [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) loaded with the initial welcome message visible.
- [ ] **Distractions Off:** Windows "Focus Assist / Do Not Disturb" turned ON; bookmarks bar hidden (`Ctrl + Shift + B`).
- [ ] **Mic Check:** Do a quick 10-second test in Loom, OBS, or Zoom and listen back to ensure your voice is crisp.
- [ ] **Cursor Movement:** Move the cursor smoothly without erratic wiggling.
- [ ] **YouTube Visibility:** When uploading, select **"Unlisted"** (not Private). Test the link in a private/incognito window before pasting into Devpost and your README.
- [ ] **Length Check:** Ensure final exported video is between 1:30 and 2:00.

## 📝 Devpost Text Submission Summary

- **Tagline:** Turning fragmented public subsidies into short, doable financial survival plans for Miami-Dade public school families.
- **Inspiration:** Single mothers in Miami face a fragmented, bilingual maze of public assistance where missing one letter cancels child care overnight.
